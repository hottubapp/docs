    Hot Tub

    /ˈhät ˌtəb/
    noun
    A large tub filled with hot, bubbling water—used for relaxing, connecting, and letting go.

    What is Hot Tub?

    Hot Tub is the first Apple-notarized adult video app built natively for iOS.

    It’s designed to give you a clean, private way to explore adult content on your terms. No account required. Limited telemetry and anonymous analytics (not tied to you); opt out in settings where available. See the Privacy Policy.

    Whether you're watching casually or curating your go-to favorites, Hot Tub makes it easy to explore without the usual friction or noise.

    Our Mission

    Hot Tub exists to build a better kind of adult platform - one that respects the people who use it and the people who create for it. The current ecosystem treats users as data and creators as inventory. We're not interested in optimizing for either of those outcomes. Our goal is simple: privacy by default, fair treatment for creators, and a product that actually works.

    We don't sell user data. We don't rely on ads or cross-site tracking. Privacy-first: limited telemetry and anonymous analytics, not tied to your identity, with opt-out where settings allow. Trust and transparency are requirements, not features.

    We believe sex work is work. That means giving creators real tools, real visibility, and real ownership. When users choose to support creators, that support goes entirely to them - we don't take a cut. The platform should enable creators, not extract from them.

    We're building for the edges, not just the mainstream. That means better discovery, advanced filtering, and support for a wide range of identities, preferences, and communities often ignored or buried on larger platforms. Users have full control over what they see - and what they avoid. Accessibility and inclusion are built in from the ground up.

    Hot Tub is a native app because quality matters. Faster, more reliable, and designed for real devices, it avoids the broken experiences of mobile web and browser workarounds. Developers can build custom sources, extend the ecosystem, or keep things private - helping Hot Tub grow as a flexible, open platform.

    This is a long-term project. Monetization is secondary to building something that doesn't compromise on privacy, fairness, or usability. Every feature is optional, and every choice is under the user's control. If we get these principles right, everything else follows.

    Why Hot Tub?

    Hot Tub puts you in control. From privacy settings to interface preferences, everything is built to let you customize your experience. Want extra discretion? You can hide or disable favorites, watch history, choose a more subtle icon, and even lock the app with FaceID, TouchID, or a passcode. Hot Tub is built to grow with you. The app supports "sources"—independent servers that provide content to the app—and "channels," which organize videos by category, theme, or provider. Anyone can build their own source using our open API and developer tools.

    Features & Benefits

    • Privacy-First Design: No accounts required. Limited telemetry; anonymous analytics not tied to you; opt out in settings—Privacy Policy.
    • Apple Notarized: Reviewed and approved for security and reliability.
    • Privacy Controls: Show or hide history, favorites, and more — plus get notified before audio plays through Bluetooth or AirPlay.
    • Optional App Lock: Use FaceID, TouchID, or a passcode to keep things private.
    • Extensible Platform: Add new sources and channels to expand your library.
    • Built for iOS: Native performance with intuitive gestures and seamless playback.
    • Clean Interface: Minimal design—no clutter, just the content you care about.
    • No Account Needed: Watch privately without ever signing up.
    • Customizable Experience: Organize favorites, adjust layout, and set preferences your way.
    • Inclusive & Respectful: Content that reflects your tastes—without assumptions.
    • Developer-Friendly: Open API and docs for building your own sources.

    Getting Started

    Download Hot Tub and start exploring—quick setup, no sign up. Check out our Install Guide to learn more about how to get started.

    Developers: Check out our Developer Docs or Join the Discord to learn more about building and hosting your own sources and channels.
